Date: 2026-10-23. Authority: `doc/plan_8.md` (original complete user plan, unchanged).

## Research question and evidence contract

Exploratory mechanism replication/adaptation, not a blind confirmatory evaluation. A: do four original ADeLe demands add transferable information beyond frozen semantic/metadata inputs and can a CPU student retain it? B: under explicitly visible peer labels, which representation/centering/regularization components explain R72 and does identifiable subject heterogeneity warrant partial pooling?

Falsifiers: reliable demands without paired source Brier improvement; gains disappearing under capacity matching/permutation; student failing to retain teacher information; slope heterogeneity indistinguishable from finite-label noise; partial pooling failing a single prespecified source comparison. Stop the corresponding family rather than expand a grid.
