A.\n\nPublished\n\n2022\n\nPublication type\n\nArticle / Letter to editor\n\n### Abstract\n\nEfficient coding and improvements in the execution order of the up-and-down-blocks algorithm for monotone or isotonic regression leads to a significant increase in speed as well as a short and simple O(n) implementation. Algorithms that use monotone regression as a subroutine, e.g., unimodal or bivariate monotone regression, also benefit from the acceleration. A substantive comparison with and characterization of currently available implementations provides an extensive overview of up-and-down-blocks implementations for the pool-adjacent-violators algorithm for simple linear ordered monotone regression.\n\nDate issued\n\n2022-05-26\n\nLanguage\n\nEnglish\n\nPublisher\n\nFoundation for Open Access Statistic\n\nIs part of\n\nJournal of Statistical Software (2022), Vol. 102, no. 1, p. 1-25\n\nhdl\n\n[1887/3515640](https://hdl.handle.net/1887/3515640)\n\ndoi\n\n[10.18637/jss.v102.c01](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v102.c01)\n\nURL\n\n[https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v102c01](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v102c01)\n\n#### Persistent identifier url\n\n[https://hdl.handle.net/1887/3515640](https://hdl.handle.net/1887/3515640)\n\n#### Documents\n\n- [01 - Busing 2022 Monotone Regression](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/download/1887/3515640/1?col=81522199280002711)\n- version: Publisher's Version\n- [Full text at Publisher's site](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v102.c01)\n- Open Access [![Licensed under Article 25fa Copyright Act/Law (Amendment Taverne)](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/resources/img//license_4.png)](https://hdl.handle.net/1887/license:4)"}
